Included example resources / Recursos de prueba incluidos

The application owner explicitly selected the following prepared demo resources
for distribution inside the single EXE:

- NAM 1 and NAM 2: the same capture supplied as "Developed by Conr4d.nam".
  These two prepared positions are identical. Initial input: -5 dB; manual
  output: -3 dB. Credit for the supplied example: conr4d / conr4daudio.
- IR1, IR2 and IR3: the supplied P00, P05 and P10 Zilla V30 examples, prepared
  with 80 Hz Low Cut and 8 kHz High Cut.

Their respective authors retain their rights. The public source snapshot does

El propietario de la aplicación indicó expresamente esos ejemplos para la
distribución integrada. Los recursos conservan los derechos de sus respectivos
autores; la licencia del programa no cambia la de las capturas o los IR.
